Get paid on the last day, even with fixes left.

Touchtape is an iPhone app for one-person trades: painters, handymen, plumbers and other contractors who work alone. It keeps each job on paper, from the estimate to the last payment.

On the last day, each fix your customer points out gets its own price. Only that amount waits; the rest is due today. When the fix is done, its price comes due. Nothing else moves.

Your customer signs the estimate on your phone, added work gets its own yes, and every step leaves a numbered paper you can send.
