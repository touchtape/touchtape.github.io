Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Touchtape keeps the records of your jobs: estimates, the customer's yes, added work, last-day lists, bills, receipts and payments. This page says what happens to that information.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone: your business details, your customers' names and contact details, job details, signatures, prices, payments, photos and the PDFs the app makes. Touchtape has no account and no server of its own. We (the developer) never receive any of it.

When information leaves your phone

Only when you send it:

  • Papers you send. When you tap Send or Text it, the PDF goes through the iPhone share sheet or Messages to whoever you choose.
  • Backups and exports. A backup or accountant export is a file you save or share yourself. Whoever holds that file can read it.
  • iCloud. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud, Apple includes the app's data in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

Doing the work in Touchtape needs a purchase through Apple: paying once, or a yearly subscription. Touchtape uses RevenueCat to check whether that purchase is on your Apple Account. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase details from Apple. It uses them to unlock the app and to show the developer purchase statistics that don't identify you. It does not receive your jobs, customers or papers. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Face ID and passcode

When you hand your phone to a customer, getting back to your screens asks for Face ID or your passcode; on an iPhone with no passcode, a 3-second hold brings you back instead. If Touchtape closes while the customer's page is open, it opens locked until the same check. The check happens in iOS; the app only learns whether it succeeded. Touchtape never sees or stores your face data or passcode.

What Touchtape does not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics of how you use the app beyond the purchase statistics described above, and no selling or sharing of your data.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes its data from your phone. Backups you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Children

Touchtape is a business tool and is not directed at children.
