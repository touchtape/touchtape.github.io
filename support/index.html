Support

Write to anilsancarr+touchtape@gmail.com. Say which iPhone and iOS version you use, and what you tapped before the problem. Please don't send your customers' details.

I paid, but the app asks me to pay again

Open Settings in Touchtape and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you paid with.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

On your iPhone, open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, choose Touchtape and tap Cancel Subscription. Apple handles the billing; the app can't cancel it for you. If you paid once, there is nothing to cancel.

My customer has the phone. How do I get back?

Tap the "Back to …" button at the top of the customer's page and use Face ID or your passcode. On an iPhone with no passcode, tap it, then press and hold it for 3 seconds. If the app was closed while the customer's page was open, it opens locked until the same check.

New phone

In Touchtape, open Settings › Back up and restore and save a backup file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone, install Touchtape, restore that file from the same screen, then tap Restore purchases.

Deleting your data

Your records live only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them. Backups you saved elsewhere stay until you delete them.
